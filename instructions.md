Here are the instructions for building this site. 

This will be a single page website with the following layout, as per the mockup in mockup.png file in the assets folder. 
- Hero section that mimics Microsoft's blue screen of death and has the message "Microsoft, your climate plan ran into a problem and needs to restart" 
- Open letter section that shows the open letter copy embedded in what looks like MS Word window document. For the actual letter, I want you to recreate the attached mockup that emulates a MS Word window on a traditional Windows background that actually shows the open letter copy. This open letter window should take up about two thirds of the width. On the right hand side there should be two buttons inviting individuals and organizations to sign on, followed by a share section that includes buttons for major social media, as well as a share image preview (which will be the blue screen of death header). Below the share section should be a  list of organizations that have endorsed the letter. Think also about how best to creatively visualize the number of signatures (20k individuals, 50 organizations).


For the two sign on buttons, they should open a popup that will show an iframe of a form. Use this form as an example for now (https://act.stand.earth/page/88799/petition/1), though there will be two different forms for the buttons down the road.
